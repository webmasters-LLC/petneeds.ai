# petneeds.ai
smarter care fot happier pets
 is a family-friendly, collaborative pet care tracker and educational platform. It is designed to help household members seamlessly coordinate daily routines, track behavior changes, assign tasks, and access expert-reviewed guidance to keep their pets happy and healthy.🚀 FeaturesFamily Dashboard: Coordinate care assignments, daily feedings, medication times, and grooming routines across multiple household members.Routine & Health Logging: Track weight, dietary habits, and physical updates to build a clear history for your local veterinarian.Educational Library: Access comprehensive, curated pet care guides for:Dogs & Cats 🐶🐱Birds, Fish, & Reptiles 🦜🐠🦎Small Pets (Hamsters, Rabbits, Guinea Pigs) 🐹Senior & Rescue Pet Management ❤️Media Gateway: Securely upload and share your pet’s milestones. All content is human-reviewed to ensure a safe, family-friendly community environment.⚠️ Educational DisclaimerPlease Note: The content provided by this application is for informational and educational purposes only. It is not a substitute for professional, licensed veterinary care, diagnostics, or treatment. Always consult your veterinarian regarding any health or medical concerns for your pet.
